Common Questions

You've Got Questions,
We've Got Answers

Everything small business owners usually ask before booking their first session.

Do I need any tech experience to benefit from AI coaching?▼
Absolutely not! In fact, most of our clients describe themselves as "not tech savvy." Our whole approach is built around plain English — no jargon, no assumptions. If you can use a smartphone, you can learn to use AI tools for your business.
How is this different from just Googling "AI tools for business"?▼
Google gives you generic information. We give you personalized coaching based on your specific business, your industry, your budget, and your goals. We cut through the overwhelming amount of information and tell you exactly what tools to use and exactly how to use them — for YOUR situation.
Will the AI tools cost me a lot of money after the session?▼
We specifically focus on free and low-cost tools wherever possible. Many of our recommended tools cost $0–$30/month. We'll always tell you the cost upfront and help you prioritize based on your budget. The goal is for AI to save you money, not cost you more.
What kind of businesses do you work with?▼
We specialize in small and local businesses — restaurants, retail shops, service businesses (plumbers, electricians, cleaners), healthcare practices, salons, boutiques, daycares, and more. If you have a small business and want to work smarter, we can help.
Can I book a session for my employees too?▼
Yes! Group sessions and team training packages are available. Contact us at stormy@arkthrive.com or mention it when booking and we'll put together a custom quote for your team.
What's your cancellation policy?▼
We understand that running a business is unpredictable. We ask for 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel. With less than 24 hours' notice, a 50% cancellation fee applies. No-shows are charged the full session fee.
How long until I see results from AI coaching?▼
Many clients start using AI tools the same day as their session and report saving time within the first week. Results depend on how quickly you implement the tools, but most business owners notice a difference immediately — especially with tasks like writing, customer communication, and scheduling.
Do I need special software or equipment?▼
Just a computer or tablet with internet access and a web browser. No special software needed. We do sessions via video call (Zoom or Google Meet), and everything we recommend can be accessed through your regular web browser.
